Map Pins

Click directly on your map image below to drop numbered room-pin labels onto the current scene - no separate tool, no coordinates to copy/paste. (An already-placed JSON export from an older session still imports fine too.) Pins are real scene items other clients see immediately - click (select) one on the map and use the "Open Room Notes" icon that appears next to it to jump to its notes (once GM Notes has a matching heading).
